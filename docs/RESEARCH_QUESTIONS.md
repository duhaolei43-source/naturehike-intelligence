# Research questions and scope

**RQ1:** What price/specification trade-offs distinguish advertised two-person backpacking tent offers accessible to U.S. consumers?
**RQ2:** How does relative U.S. search interest in selected brands/categories vary over a documented, jointly normalized Google Trends period?
**RQ3:** Which positioning and acquisition experiments follow from observable product trade-offs?

The initial comparison includes Naturehike, Kelty, REI Co-op and The North Face. MSR adds a documented lightweight structural comparison. This is a convenience sample, not a census or probability sample.

Inclusion: two-person capacity; official backpacking description; U.S./USD advertising; sufficiently documented variant-specific price and weight. Sold-out advertised listings remain included with an availability caveat. Color variants are not replicated to inflate sample size. Material variants require separately bound specifications and price.

Current pilot: 30 source-verified variant specification records across 9 brands, one collection date. The 30-variant coverage target is met. There are 27 verified display prices; three Tarptent option prices remain null rather than being replaced by a range endpoint. The exclusion log records unresolved variants and access failures. No inference about sales, market share, advertising efficiency or consumer preferences is possible from the product sample.
