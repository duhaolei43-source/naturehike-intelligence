# Source policy and publication review

| Tier | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Official product pages and manufacturer specifications | Variant-bound factual specification and observed pricing |
| B | Official industry organizations/reports | Context after scope, rights and methodology review |
| C | Google Trends / documented public statistical sources | Relative demand only within valid normalization scope |
| D | Permitted consumer discussion sources | Qualitative evidence only after permission review |

Only Tier A product facts are currently published. No paid industry report, consumer review corpus or real Trends export is included. The source registry records URL, publisher, retrieval UTC time, identifier, field list, confidence, access method, restriction review, public-use class and extraction-evidence hash.

Collection uses individual page review; no bulk crawler, CAPTCHA solving, credential bypass or anti-bot evasion exists. Official page access does not confer a general extraction license. The public dataset contains concise factual observations and links, with no copied marketing passages, photography, user review text or full-page captures. Source terms may change; downstream collectors must review the live conditions. The project does not assert blanket redistribution rights to original content.

Naturehike terms were reviewed at https://www.naturehike.com/policies/terms-of-service. Direct terms-page retrieval for several other publishers was unavailable in this pass; permission to scrape or republish their content has not been established. Consequently the collection remains a small manual factual record, and expansion must include another source-specific restriction review.

The evidence hash covers the privately retained structured extraction record, not the original HTML. No full source file was saved where rights were unclear; this limitation is explicit. Public reproduction uses the factual inputs and input hashes and does not require the private archive.

Official REI product pages are classified Tier A as official product websites, with publisher role retailer for third-party brands. This classification establishes source attribution, not independent manufacturer verification. Brand-owned versus retailer listings are explicit in product records.
