# Limitations and remaining work

- The coverage target is met with 30 source-verified specification records across 9 brands; only 27 have verified option-bound display prices. Candidates with unresolved variant binding, missing specifications or failed access are excluded. This is a research pilot.
- Official specifications are manufacturer claims; no tents were weighed or field-tested independently. Minimum/packed component definitions are not fully consistent. Source metric/imperial rounding disagreements remain visible.
- Single collection date; no trend in product pricing can be inferred. Display price is not a transaction or complete landed cost. Stock and delivery availability were not universally checked; advertised sold-out listings are included.
- Floor area is absent for three Naturehike and three Tarptent observations. Tarptent Typical weights are retained in notes rather than substituted for packed weight. Season/structure/accessory gaps exclude several products from strict cohorts. Construction labels inferred from inner tent/rainfly packages are documented derived classifications, not independent tests.
- Convenience assortment and incomplete materials controls prevent representative market, consumer preference or causal price inference. No confidence intervals or significance tests imply representative sampling.
- No real Google Trends export; RQ2 is incomplete. The synthetic fixture is engineering-only and cannot support a business claim.
- Power BI model/DAX design is UNVERIFIED. No PBIX, PBIP runtime report, report screenshots or measured DAX parity exists.
- Public-source usage is restricted to concise factual observations and attribution. No blanket permission for bulk extraction or republishing original website content is asserted. Some terms-page access failed.
- Audit evidence hashes cover structured extraction records, not original page HTML. Full desktop transcripts are not captured. The hash chain detects inconsistent edits but is not immutable or tamper-proof and cannot detect an owner rewriting the entire chain. Encrypted-backup tooling is tested; a production backup requires an owner-held passphrase and separate storage plan.
- Dashboard is local unless an explicit deployment URL is recorded. GitHub releases and CI results are claimed only when they actually exist.

Next: expand the verified sample with source-specific permission review, resolve missing comparative definitions, collect real joint-batch Trends exports, then perform customer research and authorized experiments. A supported Windows/Fabric authoring session is needed to test Power BI.
